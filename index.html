01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Maths taught me to
ask why. Robotics lets
me ask what if.

My background is in Mathematics and Physics, where I became interested in how abstract models can explain real systems.

I graduated from the University of Manchester with a First Class integrated master’s degree. My academic work has included quantum mechanics, quantum information and computation, differential geometry, and an inverse-problems project focused on image reconstruction.

I’m now developing my skills in robotics and AI. I’m especially drawn to work that connects theory with implementation: writing code, understanding sensors and systems, testing ideas, and learning from what happens outside the notebook.
