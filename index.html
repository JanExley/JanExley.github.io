01 / RESEARCH PROFILE

Maths taught me to
ask why. Robotics and
quantum ask what if.

I liked the theoretical side of Mathematics and Physics, but I also wanted to see what those ideas could do outside a notebook. That question has taken me in two directions: autonomous robotics and quantum computing.

Robotics: I’m interested in how a mobile robot can use sensor data to make decisions in a difficult environment. The gas-leak localisation research in the RAINZ CDT brings together the things I want to work on: sensing, modelling, estimation and autonomy.

Quantum computing: I’m drawn to both the mathematics of quantum algorithms and the engineering that makes a real device work. My degree has given me the chance to explore quantum information, tomography, superconducting qubits and cryogenic systems.

They’re different fields, but both make me ask the same thing: how do we get reliable information from a system we can’t observe perfectly?
